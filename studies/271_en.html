What will restore Kouta’s memory?

Wayback Machine · Japanese original

What might cause Kouta to regain his memory? I think Kouta will probably remember eventually, but what will trigger it? I've thought of a few possibilities: — Witnessing another massacre. — Being struck on the head. — Finding the letter in that bottle. — Having his brain tampered with by vectors. — A kiss from Nyu (ha ha). Regaining his memory would mean remembering the lie he told and her words asking to be killed… Is he really the only one who can stop Lucy? February 23, 2003.

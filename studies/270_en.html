Can vectors be seen?

РусскийEnglish日本語

Impressions and Analysis

I haven't checked everything carefully, so I'm not entirely sure, but the characters who appear to have been able to see vectors include the Institute staff member in EL01 (the last panel on page 25 of volume 1), Bandō, the girl who exclaimed “Oh, my!”, Tomoo, and Kanae.

I think what these people have in common is that “their lives were threatened by vectors.”

In Kanae's case, the vectors weren't aimed specifically at her, but Lucy was trying to kill the people around her indiscriminately, so I suppose she counts. In other words, perhaps the people who were there watching Lucy could see the vectors… (Among those near the edge of their range who could look straight at her without blood spray or anything else obstructing their view, was there only the man at the ten-o'clock position?)

Almost 100 percent of the people she targeted were killed. So is it a case of “the moment you see them is the moment you die”? Bandō was lucky enough to be right at the edge of their range and managed to dodge thanks to his natural reflexes. As for Kanae, perhaps she survived because there weren't enough vectors, or because someone in front of her blocked the way. …Or is that too much of a stretch?

Yuka was also struck by a vector, but there was clearly no intent to kill, so that's why she couldn't see it… That's my excuse, anyway. Does murderous intent awaken the human sixth sense…?

…I'm about to get carried away again, so I'll stop here.

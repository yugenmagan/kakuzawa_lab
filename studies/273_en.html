Special chapter “NOZOMI”

Kochi’s personal impressions

Cover

There it is! A naked baby in diapers!! Madness has taken over Manga Kakumei’s center color pages.

Giveaway

Oh, so not being able to choose a prize really was a mistake. They have extended the deadline to the 29th and want everyone to apply again. Then why not print the prizes and application rules again?? How many people still have last month’s Young Jump?? Not everyone who entered will buy this issue of Manga Kakumei, either. What happens to those who never hear about the correction and cannot reapply?? And could they not at least apologize for the mistake?

…Not that I would get angry about something like this, but I wish they were a little more considerate. (I hope next week’s YJ clears this up. Shouldn’t they announce it on the official YJ website, too?)

Just in case, I photographed the application instructions. If you no longer have the earlier YJ, please refer to these photos.

And what on earth is that “happy news”!? No, no, I’m not falling for it.

The story

“Only six months until your entrance exams”

Nozomi. Since her dad is saying this, he must mean entrance to a national university, right? So six months until the National Center Test?

“I admired my mother singing on stage”

Oh, was she an opera singer? Their house is a mansion, and her father seems very wealthy. Her grave was strangely modest, though.

“It was suicide”

Ah, another heavy subject.

“I listened to Mother’s records every day”

She must have been famous enough to have records released.

“Please forgive me!! I won’t talk back again!!”

Corporal punishment!! No… Nozomi is screaming. That is not even how a child normally apologizes. A monster… her father’s face is monstrous… And I cannot understand why he had to tie her up. Surely he knew she would wet herself right there on his lap. Instead of simply throwing the records away, he deliberately broke them in front of her. What a father. (Could this be when her wetting problem began?)

“I… I sang as hard as I could…”

Wow, Nozomi is adorable in this panel!!

“But instead she began singing on the hill behind the house”

The background seems to show somewhere near the facility where Lucy was held. So Nozomi’s house is also close to Kaede House… Which means!!

“Rustle”

Huh? That’s all?? Just that?? What about Lucy!!

“Long time no see! How are you?!”

Oh, Yuka! She is at university now, so perhaps they are meeting again after about six months? Although Nozomi wore her winter uniform when she first appeared… Well, never mind the details.

“You look even gloomier than usual”

T-that sounds pretty unnatural… Wouldn’t “Don’t you look even gloomier than usual?” be more natural? Anyway, that means they think she is always gloomy.

“♪～”

Nana!! Nana looks so cute!? Has someone taught her to read sheet music too?

“Oh, be quiet”

Why twice? Ah, to make up for how little attention the character gets!! …But it seems “Well then, Nozomi, I’ll walk you to the entrance” is Kouta’s line*1. So who says “Oh, be quiet”??

“Good luck”

Nyu, Nana, and Mayu have no dialogue in this chapter, but this panel alone is enough… right?

“I will live the way I choose”

Way to go, Nozomi!!

“Dramatic soprano”

How do you tell voice types apart by listening? It seems different from vocal range. I know nothing about classical singing. But Bando is a castrato.

“Number 70119”

What does that mean? Or does it mean nothing? Which is it?

Impressions

For Mr. Okamoto, this felt rather conventional, and I thought it lacked a particularly strong message. Still, I am satisfied that Nozomi seems likely to find happiness.

Nozomi’s father’s harshness was love expressed backwards… although it seemed far too harsh to me. At any rate, he accepted her choice, so perhaps it is a happy outcome for now. Of course, we do not know whether she will enter music college, become an opera singer, or damage her throat. That kind of happy ending, with no promise of a bright future, reminds me somewhat of Digitopolis.

But if she is taking entrance exams, this takes place after EL72, right? So everyone is safe at least until spring? Then what was Anna’s prophecy supposed to mean??

Nana must often have been naked at the research facility, yet is she now the most sensitive to cold? She is wearing a padded hanten jacket again, and trousers too. The exact opposite of Mayu, who still gets by with a single sweater. I love that Nana looks the most domestically dressed of them all.

Finally

Wanta, who appeared in just one panel, has shrunk again.

Additional note

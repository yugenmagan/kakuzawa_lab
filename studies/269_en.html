What Happened to Kanae

Impressions and Analysis

Let us speculate about what happened to Kanae (the subject also came up on the message board).

First, here are the key pieces of information…

“I'd love to go to the sea, just once…” (EL02)
“Kanae will never be able to leave this room again.” (EL02)
“I think that was the year… Dad died in a traffic accident and Kanae fell ill.” (EL09)
“Sorry for barging in every summer.” (EL33)
  • Kouta received a seashell from Kanae. (EL02)
  • Kanae does not appear in Yuka's flashbacks in EL07 and EL08.
  • EL33 apparently takes place after the flashback in EL08 (judging from the line about “cutout shapes”).

There are so many mysteries that no firm conclusion is possible at this point…

The stumbling block is the line “I'd like to go to the sea” in the bedside scene (EL02). If Kanae had never been to the sea by the time she was hospitalized, how could she have given Kouta a seashell? That is where the chronology seems to go wrong. But things fit together rather neatly if the scene is not “Kanae speaking to Kouta from her hospital bed,” but rather “Kouta looking at his sister lying in a hospital bed and recalling an earlier conversation.” (Of course, I may be completely wrong.)

I also suspect that he was told it was an “illness,” rather than an “accident,” because Kanae really had been infected with that virus.

12 February 2003

Addendum

This analysis was written using the information available at the time of EL33. As of now (Sunday, 7 September 2003, 06:46:09), I believe the setup involving the seashell was subsequently changed.

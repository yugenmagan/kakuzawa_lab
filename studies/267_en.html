Elfen Lied Day

Wayback Machine · Japanese original

Today is Hinamatsuri, the Peach Blossom Festival. For Kochi, who has only brothers, it has been utterly irrelevant since birth (needless to say, Kochi is male too). Speaking of repeating numbers, let's remember: March 3 — Hinamatsuri (Peach Blossom Festival). May 5 — Children's Day (Boys' Festival). June 6 — Elfen Lied Day (Day of Terror). Of course, June 6 is the release date of Young Jump 2002 No. 27, which carried the first installment of the serialized Elfen Lied. And “Day of Terror” isn't just a joke: apparently it comes from the number 666 in Revelation. …Could this be a coincidence? March 3, 2003.

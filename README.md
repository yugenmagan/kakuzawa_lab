# Kakuzawa Laboratory

A digital preservation project dedicated to restoring Kakuzawa Laboratory (角沢研究室(仮)), an early Japanese fan website devoted to Lynn Okamoto's Elfen Lied.

The archive preserves original Japanese articles, provides Russian and English translations, and reconstructs historical fan research and real-world location guides, including a pilgrimage through Kamakura and Enoshima.

The goal is to preserve a small piece of early-2000s Japanese anime and manga internet culture.
